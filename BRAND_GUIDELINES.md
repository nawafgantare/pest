# Brand Guidelines for Shakti Pest Control

## 1. Brand Overview
Shakti Pest Control is a trusted pest management provider established in 1983. The brand identity is grounded in safety, reliability, eco-friendliness, and professional expertise across residential, commercial, and industrial establishments.

## 2. Logo & Visual Identity
- **Logo Asset**: `assets/images/spc-logo.png`
- **Logo Description**: Clean emblem combining bold typography with emblem iconography representing protection and pest elimination.

## 3. Brand Color Palette

| Usage | Color Name | Hex Code | RGB | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary** | Shakti Deep Red / Crimson | `#9E1B1E` | `rgb(158, 27, 30)` | Brand hero color for CTA buttons, header topbars, and highlighted titles |
| **Secondary** | Shield Dark Navy / Slate | `#1A2530` | `rgb(26, 37, 48)` | Used for dark sections, footers, primary headings, and navigation menus |
| **Accent / Highlight** | Bright Eco Green | `#2E7D32` | `rgb(46, 125, 50)` | Accent color for safety badges, eco-friendly guarantees, and success alerts |
| **Supporting Accent** | Warm Amber / Gold | `#F5A623` | `rgb(245, 166, 35)` | Used for star ratings, badges, and attention alerts |
| **Background Light** | Clean Ice Off-White | `#F8F9FA` | `rgb(248, 249, 250)` | Section backgrounds, light cards, and subtle contrasts |
| **Text Primary** | Deep Charcoal | `#212529` | `rgb(33, 37, 41)` | Body font text color |
| **Text Muted** | Neutral Grey | `#6C757D` | `rgb(108, 117, 125)` | Subtitles, meta info, and form helper text |

## 4. Typography
- **Primary Body Font**: `'Poppins', 'Segoe UI', system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif`
- **Heading Font**: `'Inter', 'Roboto', 'Segoe UI', sans-serif`
- **Font Weights**:
  - Regular: `400`
  - Medium: `500`
  - Semi-Bold: `600`
  - Bold: `700`
